   WARN  32 of 243 codons (13.2%, against about 3.4% in an average E. coli

         (Pro) x8, GGA (Gly) x5, AGA (Arg) x4. That is a high enough load

         tRNAs, such as Rosetta, is the cheaper fix than resynthesising the
         gene

         20. A stall this early aborts the product rather than slowing it,

-- colony PCR screen -----------------------------------------------------
  polymerase     Taq

  5' junction
    asks         is the insert joined to the vector at its 5' end, the right way round?
    primers      colVecF (3' end 5062) x colInsR (3' end 5527)
    clone        500 bp
    parental     no product  <- a band can only come from a real clone
     note  the parental vector gives no product, so a band means the insert is joined to the vector and not merely present in the colony

  3' junction
    asks         is the insert joined to the vector at its 3' end?
    primers      colInsF (3' end 5795) x colVecR (3' end 264)
    clone        536 bp
    parental     no product  <- a band can only come from a real clone
     note  the parental vector gives no product, so a band means the insert is joined to the vector and not merely present in the colony

  flanking
    asks         empty or full, by size, in one reaction
    primers      colVecF (3' end 5062) x colVecR (3' end 264)
    clone        1269 bp
    parental     607 bp
     note  uncut or religated parental vector gives 607 bp, which is the band a negative colony shows

  primers to order:
    colVecF    5'-TGATGTCGGCGATATAGG-3'   18 nt  Tm 57.5
    colInsR    5'-GGAGAGCCAGTCAGATTG-3'   18 nt  Tm 59.0
    colInsF    5'-AGATCCCAAGGCTCAAAG-3'   18 nt  Tm 58.2
    colVecR    5'-GAAGGGAAGAAAGCGAAAG-3'   19 nt  Tm 57.8

   WARN  colony PCR shows that a junction exists, not that the sequence is right. It cannot see a point mutation introduced by the cloning PCR, so a positive colony still has to be sequenced
   note  each junction pair has one primer in the vector backbone and one in the insert. A pair with both primers inside the insert would amplify leftover template and PCR product carried through the ligation just as well as a real recombinant, so every colony would score positive
   note  a junction pair also reports orientation: an insert ligated backwards gives no product at all, whereas a flanking vector pair gives the same size either way
   note  Taq is the usual choice here: fidelity does not matter for a screen, and tolerance of crude lysate does. Lyse the colony first, or the early cycles go on releasing template
